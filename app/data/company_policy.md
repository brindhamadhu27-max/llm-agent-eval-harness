# Acme Corp Employee Policy Handbook (fictional - for testing only)

## Leave Policy
Casual leave: every employee gets 12 days of casual leave per year.
Sick leave: every employee gets 8 days of sick leave per year, and a medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.
Earned leave: every employee gets 18 days of earned leave per year, and unused earned leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days.
Maternity leave: eligible employees get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.
Paternity leave: eligible employees get 10 working days of paternity leave.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 working days in advance through the HR portal, except for sick leave.

## Expense Policy
Meal allowance: employees on business travel get a meal allowance of INR 1,500 per day.
Hotel limit: hotel stays are reimbursed up to INR 8,000 per night in metro cities.
Claim deadline: expense claims must be submitted with original receipts within 30 days of the expense.
Approval: any single expense claim above INR 25,000 needs director approval.
Air travel: economy class is mandatory for flights shorter than 6 hours.

## Work From Home Policy
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Work from home is not allowed during the first 90 days after joining.
Employees working from home must be available on the company chat during core hours of 11 AM to 4 PM.

## Information Security Policy
Minimum password length is 14 characters, and passwords must be changed every 90 days.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts.
All company laptops must have full disk encryption enabled.
Security incidents must be reported to the security team within 24 hours at security@acme-example.com.
Employees must never share their passwords or access tokens with anyone, including colleagues.
